### Overview

Recycool is a serious game built for teaching kids (and adults) how to recycle (with style). Wishcycling and improper waste disposal leads to 90% of recyclable materials being sent to landfills within Salt Lake City. This game lets players perform trickshots with trash as they learn the proper way to recycle according to Salt Lake City guidelines.

### Overview

For this project, our team was challenged to create an arcade-inspired game based on a title released before 1984. We took inspiration from the classic arcade racing game Nürburgring 1 (in 1976 which later inspired Night Driver if you heard of that one) and gave the concept a completely different—and much more chaotic—direction.

Jaywalk Justice puts you behind the wheel with one simple objective: hit the streets, drive fast, and rack up points by taking out jaywalkers as they cross. Controversial, right? That's what Rapid Prototyping gives us, a chance to experiment and be radical with our decisions. It’s fast, ridiculous, metal, and designed to capture the quick, pick-up-and-play feeling of classic arcade games while putting our own spin on the original racing game.
